§ II   The Disciplines

Four things,done properly.

II

We do not offer everything. We offer four disciplines, and we practise each of them as though our name depended on it. It does.

  1. I.

    Stewardship

    The careful keeping of what matters, so that it may be handed on in better condition than it was received.

    →
  2. II.

    Acquisition

    The patient sourcing of the rare, the right and the enduring. We would rather wait a year than settle for a season.

    →
  3. III.

    Counsel

    Quiet advice, given once and given well. Our discretion is not a policy; it is a habit of several lifetimes.

    →
  4. IV.

    Commission

    Bespoke work made by hand, to a single brief, by people who sign their names inside it.

    →
§The Manner of Engagement

How one begins.

i.

An introduction

Most of our clients arrive through someone they trust. If you have no such person, a well-written letter will do.

ii.

A conversation

Unhurried, in person where possible, and entirely in confidence. We listen considerably more than we speak.

iii.

A commitment

Should we both wish to proceed, we agree terms simply and plainly, and then we get on with it.
