Gmail (on a computer)

  1. Open Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "Crownsmere".
  3. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and for replies.
  5. Click Save Changes at the very bottom of the page.

Outlook (web and the new Outlook)

  1. Open Settings, then Account (or Accounts), then Signatures.
  2. Click New signature, name it "Crownsmere" and paste into the box.
  3. Choose it as the default for new messages and replies, then Save.

Apple Mail (Mac)

  1. Open this page in Safari and click Copy signature.
  2. In Mail, open Settings, then Signatures, choose the account and click +.
  3. Untick Always match my default message font, then paste into the right-hand box.

iPhone and iPad

The iPhone's Mail signature setting keeps only plain text, so use this version there: Settings, Apps, Mail, Signature.
