§ I   The House

A house,not a brand.

I

Crownsmere began with a simple conviction: that the best things in life are chosen carefully, kept properly, and passed on quietly.

An accountOf ourselves

Every great house has a way of doing things. Ours is unhurried. We take on few commitments, and we keep every one of them. We prefer a handshake to a contract, though we are perfectly happy to provide both.

We believe that taste is not the same as expense, that patina is earned rather than bought, and that the most important conversations happen in rooms without an audience.

Our clients come to us for different reasons, but they tend to stay for the same one: we treat what they care about as though it were our own, and we never speak of it elsewhere.

§Rules of the House

Three rules, kept without exception.

Rule the first

Never raise one’s voice.

Not in the room, not in the market, and not in our advertising, of which there is none.

Rule the second

Buy once, buy well.

The finest thing is usually the one you never need to replace. We will tell you when to wait.

Rule the third

What is said here, stays here.

Discretion is the oldest luxury there is. It is also the one we guard most closely.
