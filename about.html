WhyCrownsmere.

A personal, discreet and one-to-one property service, built around the client rather than the listing.

As an independent consultant, I work exclusively for you. My clients come to me with a request, and my role is simple: to find what they are looking for, and to look after their interests at every step.

Property is about people, not just buildings. I take on a small number of clients so that each one receives my full attention, honest advice and a search shaped entirely around them.

You will never be passed between departments or shown a property simply because it needs selling. From the first conversation to the final signature, you deal directly with me, in complete confidence.
