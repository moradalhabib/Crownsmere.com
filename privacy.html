Privacy notice

Your privacy, respected.

Discretion is at the heart of what we do. This notice explains, plainly, what happens to the information you share with Crownsmere Estate.

Last updated 23 October 2026

Who we are

Crownsmere Estate is a private property consultancy based in London, United Kingdom. Its principal is Alaa Qadir, Senior Property Consultant. For the purposes of UK data protection law, Crownsmere Estate is the controller of the personal information described here. You can contact us about privacy at any time at hello@crownsmere.com.

What we collect

Only what you choose to share: your name, email address and telephone number, how and when you prefer to be contacted, and the details of your request (for example the kind of property you are looking for, preferred areas, budget and timeframe). Student enquiries may also include your university, your housing and guarantor situation and whether you have found a property. If you write to us or call, we also keep that correspondence. Please do not submit passport details, bank statements or identity documents through these public enquiry forms.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your brief.
  • To carry out the search or other services you ask us to provide.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence.
  • To discuss potential professional guarantor services and rental options when you ask for student assistance.

We rely on your request for us to act before any agreement is made, our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, and, where you give it on our forms, your agreement to be contacted. We never use your details for unrelated marketing, and we never sell your personal data.

If you ask us to introduce you to an estate agent or a professional guarantor provider, we will explain the proposed introduction and request your agreement before sharing information with them. Those firms may then act as independent controllers of the information you supply to them. Crownsmere may receive a disclosed referral commission from a participating partner. An enquiry does not automatically apply for a guarantor, guarantee a rental property, or authorise us to share your details with such partners.

Who helps us

A small number of trusted service providers process information on our behalf, only to provide their service:

  • Your chosen email provider sends the email you choose to send from your mail application. We do not automatically receive or store incomplete website form entries.
  • Our email hosting provider stores the emails you send us.
  • AI-assisted email filtering may be used for spam screening if enabled for our hello@crownsmere.com mailbox. Where active, messages may be processed by Anthropic for that limited purpose.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that is the case, information is transferred with appropriate safeguards in place. With your separate agreement, we may also share relevant details with others involved in your matter, such as solicitors, agents or professional guarantor providers, when that is needed to help you.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, including any legal, accounting or regulatory requirements, and then delete it securely.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Just email hello@crownsmere.com. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Its fonts, images and scripts are all loaded from this website; nothing is requested from third parties. Your draft enquiries remain in your browser until you send them from your own mail application.

Security

This website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and uses a strict content security policy. To report a security concern, please email hello@crownsmere.com.

Changes

If this notice changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
